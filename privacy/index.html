Privacy

What this concept site does with your information. Last updated November 4, 2026.

Who made this site

This is a design concept built by WhittWorks Studios (whittworkstudios.com) from the public content of alphaedu.pro. It is not the official Alpha EDU site and is not run by Alpha EDU.

The booking form

The form on the Book Alpha EDU page is a working demo. When you press send, it checks your entries in your browser, shows “Demo only. Nothing was sent.”, and clears itself. Nothing you type is sent to anyone or stored anywhere.

Information collected automatically

The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and stores nothing on your device. Fonts, images, and videos are served from this site, not from a third party. If the concept is hosted for review, the host may keep standard server logs (IP address, time, and page requested) as part of running the service.

External links

The Resources page and the footer link to other sites, such as College Scorecard and Instagram. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect information from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this note is posted on this page with a new date at the top.
